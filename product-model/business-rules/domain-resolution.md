# The domain field validates the inspected dot-containing form and refuses another identity’s configured domain; no DNS ownership proof or ordinary-settings Premium gate occurs

The domain field validates the inspected dot-containing form and refuses another identity’s configured domain; no DNS ownership proof or ordinary-settings Premium gate occurs. Retirement eligibility includes grandfathered redirects. Host routing treats an owner’s username host differently from non-owner custom-domain redirects; unknown subdomains yield not found and unknown custom hosts yield bad request.
